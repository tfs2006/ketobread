Quick Fixes

5-Second Tips That Make a Huge Difference

🌡️ Room Temp Eggs

Cold eggs don't incorporate well. Let eggs sit out 30 minutes before baking for better texture.

⚖️ Weigh Don't Scoop

Scooping flour adds 20-30% more than needed. Use a kitchen scale for consistent results.

⏰ Cool Completely

Cutting warm keto bread = crumbly mess. Wait until FULLY cool (45+ min) before slicing.

🧈 Add Fat for Moisture

Keto bread dries out fast. Extra butter or oil keeps it moist and improves mouthfeel.

🍳 Separate Your Eggs

Whipping egg whites separately creates air pockets for lighter, fluffier bread.

🧪 Fresh Baking Powder

Old baking powder = flat bread. Test it: drop in water—if it doesn't fizz, replace it.
