Why Is My Keto Bread Gummy? Fix the Wet, Rubbery Center

Last updated: March 2026

Gummy keto bread is one of the most misleading failures because it can seem almost done at first. The crust looks acceptable, the loaf smells finished, but the center stays wet, rubbery, or sticky after slicing.

This usually happens when the loaf carries more moisture than the structure can set before the outside browns.

Need to diagnose the cause fast? Use the Keto Bread Problem Solver to check whether your gummy center was caused by pan size, bake time, flour choice, or a quick-bread formula pushed too far.

Why Keto Bread Turns Gummy

Underbaking

This is the most common cause. Keto loaves often need a little longer than people expect, especially when the batter is rich in eggs, cheese, or moisture-heavy ingredients.

The Loaf Is Too Deep

If the pan is deep or the loaf is scaled up from a smaller formula, the outside can look done while the middle still needs more time.

Too Much Moisture for the Formula

Extra eggs, too much liquid, or a flour blend that cannot absorb enough moisture can all leave the center under-set.

Judging It Before It Cools

Keto bread often improves a lot after cooling. Steam escapes, the crumb sets, and some of the tacky feeling disappears. But if the loaf stays gummy when fully cool, cooling alone will not save it.

How to Fix a Gummy Center

  • Bake longer at a moderate temperature: Give the center time to set.
  • Use a shallower loaf or smaller batch: Reduce the distance heat has to travel.
  • Do not scale mug bread into a loaf: Quick formulas often fail when enlarged.
  • Cool before slicing: Let the crumb finish setting naturally.
  • Adjust the formula if needed: Too much moisture or binder can keep the center tacky.

When Gummy Bread Is Really a Structure Problem

Sometimes a gummy center is a symptom of weak structure rather than pure underbaking. If the loaf cannot support its own moisture, the center stays heavy and sticky even when the outside is done. That is when a better formula matters more than another few minutes in the oven.
